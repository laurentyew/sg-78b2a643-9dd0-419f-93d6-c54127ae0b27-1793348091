## Notes
Improve the landing page for mobile-first reading. Prioritize faster understanding, cleaner vertical rhythm, more comfortable font sizing, and easier scanning across the hero, audience panel, waitlist, features, comparison, pricing, and FAQ sections. Keep the desktop design direction intact while making the small-screen experience feel intentionally designed rather than simply compressed.

## Checklist
- [ ] Review the global typography and spacing rules used across the landing page
- [ ] Tighten hero heading, subheading, proof cards, and CTA layout for easier mobile scanning
- [ ] Improve section spacing and card padding across audience, features, waitlist, comparison, pricing, and FAQ blocks
- [ ] Reduce dense copy areas and rebalance text sizes for headings, body copy, labels, and supporting text
- [ ] Improve mobile readability of comparison/pricing layouts so users can understand value without horizontal strain
- [ ] Preserve SEO coverage and conversion messaging while simplifying the reading flow

## Acceptance
On mobile, the landing page feels easier to scan, section spacing is more consistent, and font sizes support quick understanding without looking cramped or oversized.
