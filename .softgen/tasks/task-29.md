## Notes
Localized pages rank only if they earn locale-relevant signals. This task covers the off-page and measurement work that makes the three language trees competitive, not the on-page build (tasks 26–28).

Measurement: Search Console per-locale query reporting (URL prefix filter), plus a monthly comparison of impressions per language. Without this you cannot tell whether zh-Hant is winning Taiwan or losing to a competitor.

Off-page: Malaysian / Singaporean business directories and startup listings (Malay and Chinese descriptions), plus marketing-community and blog outreach in each language. Quality over volume — 10 relevant localized links beat 200 directory spam links.

Honesty constraints from PRODUCT_FACTS.md apply to every asset: no fabricated customer counts, no invented awards, competitor claims verified with a last-checked date.

## Checklist
- [ ] Monthly per-language reporting template: impressions, clicks, average position and top queries for the en, ms, zh-hant and zh-hans URL prefixes
- [ ] Ten localized business-directory and startup-listing citations with unique Malay and Chinese descriptions and a link to the correct locale home page
- [ ] Five Malaysian SME / e-commerce / marketing blogs approached with a Malay-language guest or mention pitch
- [ ] Five Taiwan / Hong Kong marketing-community pitches in Traditional Chinese
- [ ] Five Malaysia / Singapore Chinese-business pitches in Simplified Chinese
- [ ] Launch announcement copy in all four languages for partner and customer newsletters
- [ ] 90-day localized content calendar: ten long-tail article titles per language, each with target keyword, search intent and the page it should link to
- [ ] Quarterly hreflang and 404 audit on locale URLs using the existing SEO audit script

## Acceptance
- Search Console shows distinct query sets and impressions for each language prefix within 30 days of launch.
- At least ten live, locale-relevant referring domains point at the locale home pages rather than the English home.
